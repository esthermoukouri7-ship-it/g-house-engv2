05The useful bits

Good questions.
Straight answers.

A few things people often ask before we get started. Still curious? We’re always happy to talk it through.

Ask us something else↗
What kinds of projects do you take on?

We work across homes, small businesses and community spaces, from a single repair or safety check to a full rewire, new build or lighting plan. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll let you know honestly if we’re the right fit.

Can I get an estimate before committing?

Of course. We’ll talk through the scope first, then provide a clear written estimate with what’s included. If a site visit is needed to price it properly, we’ll arrange one before any work is agreed.

Are your electricians qualified and insured?

Yes. Work is carried out by qualified, insured electricians and tested to the applicable standards. We provide the relevant certification and explain any findings when the job is complete.

Do you install EV chargers and smart-home systems?

We do. We can advise on charger locations, capacity and practical controls, then plan an installation around your property and how you use it. We’ll check what’s suitable before recommending equipment.

What happens if I have an electrical emergency?

Call us and explain what’s happening. If there’s smoke, a fire or immediate danger, move to safety and contact your local emergency services first. Don’t touch damaged wiring or equipment that may still be live.
